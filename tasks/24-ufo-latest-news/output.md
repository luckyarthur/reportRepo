## Government & official activity

### 1. AARO FY2025 Consolidated Annual Report on UAP

- **What:** The statutorily-required annual report from the DoD's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office. It documents **319 UAP reports** received between **2024-06-02 and 2025-05-30** — 284 concerning events inside the reporting period, 35 concerning events from 2010–2024. AARO **resolved 114** of the 319, and separately **closed 256 cases** carried over from earlier periods, for **370 resolutions** in total. Reported attributions: balloons, birds, satellites, aircraft, unmanned aerial systems, and one instance each of a rocket launch and a manned jet pack.
- **Who / when:** AARO, released on or about **2026-07-21** — reported as being months after the statutory deadline.
- **Why it matters:** This is the authoritative government count for the period and the baseline against which every other claim in the window should be read. It also contains the window's single most-discussed operational case: a report from **U.S. Navy assets off the coast of Virginia** describing **approximately 100 airborne UAP and two likely uncrewed surface systems**. Note carefully: AARO **investigating** that report is not AARO **confirming** anything about its nature.
- **Sources:** [AARO FY2025 Consolidated Annual Report (PDF, primary)](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/FY25%20UAP%20Annual%20Report/AARO_FY2025_Consolidated_Annual_Report_on_UAP.pdf); [The Black Vault](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/aaro-releases-fy2025-uap-report-months-after-statutory-deadline-citing-319-new-cases-and-expanded-satellite-analysis/); [DefenseScoop, 2026-07-21](https://defensescoop.com/2026/07/21/pentagon-investigating-ufo-uap-event-near-virginia-coast/).

### 2. ODNI preliminary guidance on UAP disclosure — NDA waivers

- **What:** A memorandum providing preliminary guidance for routing all UAP information to the **PURSUE task force** for review and declassification. Its operative provision: the Department of War and the Intelligence Community will implement a process under which **prior non-disclosure agreements, oaths, and commitments are waived** for current and former government employees and contractors speaking with authorized representatives of AARO or PURSUE. All IC members were directed to designate a PURSUE point of contact **within 30 days**.
- **Who / when:** Office of the Director of National Intelligence, **2026-07-31**.
- **Why it matters:** This is the window's most structurally significant development. The recurring obstacle in UAP oversight has been that potential witnesses cite classification obligations; a formal waiver channel changes the mechanics of testimony rather than adding to the pile of released documents. Its practical effect is not yet observable — **no testimony resulting from this waiver had been reported by the close of the window.**
- **Sources:** [DLA Piper analysis, 2026-08](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/08/odni-issues-preliminary-guidance-on-disclosure-of-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena); [Douglas Messier, Parabolic Arc](https://douglasmmessier.substack.com/p/guidance-published-for-uap-disclosure).

### 3. PURSUE fourth release

- **What:** 40 newly declassified files, **19 of them videos**, covering early Cold War intelligence files, nuclear-site records, Navy "Range Fouler" reports, modern military sensor video, NASA spaceflight material, and Project Blue Book oversight records. Specific items reported include imagery from the **1996 NASA STS-80** Space Shuttle Columbia mission, a 2019 sighting in the Eastern United States described by an observer with 28 years of combined Air Force and Navy service, and unresolved 2020 reports over the Western U.S. and the Atlantic.
- **Who / when:** Department of War, **2026-07-10**, published to WAR.GOV/UFO.
- **Why it matters:** Continues the rolling cadence established in May. The provenance of the historical material is documentary; the **modern sensor videos remain unresolved, which means unexplained, not anomalous in origin.**
- **Sources:** [Department of War release (primary)](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4539898/department-of-war-publishes-fourth-release-of-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena/); [GlobalSecurity.org mirror, 2026-07-10](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/07/mil-260710-dod03.htm); [NewsNation](https://www.newsnationnow.com/space/ufo/pentagon-ufo-files-fourth-release/); [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/pentagon-releases-new-batch-of-uap-videos-and-historical-files-but-clarity-remains-elusive/).

### 4. PURSUE fifth release

- **What:** **41 files** — documents, images, and videos — sourced from the Pentagon, FBI, CIA, State Department, and the Executive Office of the President. **Oldest record 1950; most recent 2026.** Reported contents include a military pilot's account of a large triangle that "blocked out the stars" over **Afghanistan in 2002**; **FBI-created renderings** based on firsthand witness accounts; and an intelligence report stating that personnel aboard a gunship "observed approximately 25 instances of UAPs while conducting a live fire exercise in the **Gulf of Oman**," described as "cold orbs flying at lower altitudes between 250 to 1300 MPH in different formations as well as maneuvering aggressively," which "appeared to react to the aircraft firing its main cannon."
- **Who / when:** Department of War, **2026-08-07**.
- **Why it matters:** The Gulf of Oman report is the most operationally striking narrative released in the window. It is a **documented military report of what personnel observed** — that is a real, sourced fact about a report's contents. It is **not** a determination of what the objects were, and the release does not characterize them.
- **Sources:** [CBS News, 2026-08-07](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ufo-files-pentagon-5th-release-documents-videos/); [CNN, 2026-08-07](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/07/us/video/pentagon-ufo-files-video-fbi-renderings-vrtc); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/video/pentagon-releases-fifth-batch-of-uap-files-267979333691); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/new-ufo-files-details-fbi-records-of-2026-sightings-12296595).

## Science & research

### Galileo Project — distance measurement and the Las Vegas observatory

- **What:** Work on **distance measurement** as the discriminating observable for UAP characterization, analyzed by Galileo postdoctoral researcher **Dr. Regina Sarmiento**. The project's newest observatory, in **Las Vegas**, comprises three units arranged in a triangle with legs of **10.2 km, 10.1 km, and 2.0 km**, instrumented across infrared, visible, radio, and audio bands. The multi-station geometry is what makes triangulated range — and therefore true size and speed — recoverable, which single-sensor military video cannot provide.
- **Venue and status:** Reported via **Avi Loeb's Medium posts (July 2026)** — **self-published, not peer-reviewed.** The Galileo Project has a genuine peer-reviewed record in the *Journal of Astronomical Instrumentation*, but that body of work covers methodology and instrumentation. **As of this window, no peer-reviewed Galileo paper reports a definitive UAP identification.**
- **Why it matters:** It is the clearest methodological critique available of the government-disclosure track: releasing more unresolved sensor video does not resolve anything, because the instruments were never designed to constrain range. **No new peer-reviewed UAP result was identified as published inside this window.**
- **Sources:** [Avi Loeb, "A Distance Measurement is Worth a Thousand Words," 2026-07](https://avi-loeb.medium.com/a-distance-measurement-is-worth-a-thousand-words-regarding-the-nature-of-uap-72c99e900636); [Galileo Project peer-reviewed papers in JAI](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/galileo/news/galileo-project-publishes-first-peer-reviewed-scientific-papers-jai); [Journal of Astronomical Instrumentation overview](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/S2251171723400032).

## Debunkings & corrections

This section is not a counterweight added for balance — in this window the deflationary material is quantitatively the larger part of the official record.

- **370 case resolutions, all prosaic.** AARO's FY2025 report resolved 114 new cases and closed 256 older ones; **every resolved case received a conventional explanation.** The attribution list is mundane: balloons, birds, satellites, aircraft, drones, one rocket launch, one manned jet pack. ([AARO FY2025 report](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/FY25%20UAP%20Annual%20Report/AARO_FY2025_Consolidated_Annual_Report_on_UAP.pdf))
- **No verifiable extraterrestrial evidence — restated.** AARO's position through five PURSUE tranches and hundreds of investigated reports remains that it has found **no verifiable evidence that any case involved extraterrestrial beings, activity, or technology.** ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/pentagon-says-no-verifiable-evidence-extraterrestrial-technology-beings-activity); [SpaceDaily](https://spacedaily.com/t-pentagon-five-uap-file-releases-unresolved-no-extraterrestrial-evidence/))
- **"Unresolved" is an evidence gap, not a finding.** Reporting in the window explicitly flagged the definitional trap: **unresolved means unknown, not unexplained-therefore-anomalous.** Most unresolved cases lack sufficient sensor data to resolve either way. ([SpaceDaily](https://spacedaily.com/t-pentagon-five-uap-file-releases-unresolved-no-extraterrestrial-evidence/))
- **Several released PURSUE videos were assessed as consistent with balloons or sensor artifacts** — i.e. material released as "UAP files" includes footage with prosaic assessments attached.
- **"Data alone is not disclosure."** Parts of the UAP research community criticized the PURSUE releases as volume without resolution — a documented critique of the disclosure program's substance. ([DefenseScoop, 2026-05-14](https://defensescoop.com/2026/05/14/uap-trump-first-pursue-ufo-file-drop/))

## Cross-cutting themes

Three patterns define this window. **First, the disclosure program shifted from documents to procedure.** Tranches four and five added 81 files to a growing archive, but the ODNI's 2026-07-31 NDA-waiver guidance is the item most likely to change what becomes knowable — it targets the testimonial bottleneck rather than the paper one. Whether it produces anything is an open question with a natural test window over the next several months.

**Second, volume and resolution moved in opposite directions.** Five tranches and 319 fresh reports produced zero cases with an established non-prosaic explanation, and 370 resolutions that were all conventional. The pattern is a widening archive of unresolved material alongside a stable official finding of nothing verifiable — which is why the "data alone is not disclosure" critique gained traction among researchers who are themselves sympathetic to the subject.

**Third, the methodological critique sharpened.** The Galileo Project's emphasis on distance measurement identifies precisely why the government archive cannot settle anything: military sensors recover bearing, not range, so size and speed remain unconstrained. More footage from such instruments cannot resolve what more footage was never able to resolve.
